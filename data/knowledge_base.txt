
COLLEGE INFORMATION

College Name: Oriental Institute of Science & Technology

ACADEMIC RULES

Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75%.
Students with attendance below 75% may be detained from the semester examination.
Students should regularly attend lectures, practical sessions, and tutorials.

EXAMINATION

The college conducts internal assessments during each semester.
Students must complete their internal assessment requirements before appearing for the final semester examination.
The final semester examination is conducted at the end of each semester.

COURSES

The Computer Science and Engineering department offers undergraduate courses in Computer Science.
Students study subjects including Data Structures, Database Management Systems, Operating Systems,
Computer Networks, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Software Engineering.

PLACEMENT

The college provides placement assistance to eligible final-year students.
Students may participate in recruitment drives conducted by companies visiting the campus.
Placement eligibility can depend on academic performance, backlogs, and company-specific requirements.

LIBRARY

The college library provides textbooks, reference books, journals, and digital learning resources.
Students can borrow books using their college identity card.

STUDENT SUPPORT

Students can contact their respective department office for academic queries.
Students can contact the placement cell for placement-related queries.
Students can contact the examination department for examination-related queries.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement?
A: Students are required to maintain at least 75% attendance.

Q: Who handles placement activities?
A: The college placement cell handles placement activities.

Q: Where should students go for examination-related questions?
A: Students should contact the examination department.

Q: What department handles academic queries?
A: Students should contact their respective department office.

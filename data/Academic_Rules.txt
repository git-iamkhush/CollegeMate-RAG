
ACADEMIC RULES

Attendance:
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% during the semester.
Students with attendance below 75% may be detained from the semester examination.
Attendance is calculated separately for lectures, tutorials, and practical sessions.

Examinations:
The college conducts internal assessments during each semester.
Students must complete all required internal assessments before appearing for the final semester examination.
The final semester examination is conducted at the end of each semester.

Academic Performance:
Students are expected to complete assignments, practical work, internal assessments, and examinations according to the academic calendar.

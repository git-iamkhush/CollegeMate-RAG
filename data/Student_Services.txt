
STUDENT SERVICES

Library:
The college library provides textbooks, reference books, journals, and digital learning resources.
Students can borrow books using their college identity card.

Academic Support:
Students should contact their respective department office for academic queries.

Examination Support:
Students should contact the examination department for examination-related queries.

Placement Support:
Students should contact the placement cell for placement-related queries.

Student Identity:
Students should carry their college identity card when using college services.
